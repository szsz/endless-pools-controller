Small pixel fonts — number legibility on a 64×64 HUB75 panel

Every Adafruit_GFX font small enough for a 64px-tall list (glyph height ≤ 8px), plus the built-in 6×8 GLCD font as a baseline. Rendered with the same glyph decoder hub75.cpp uses — GFX fonts are placed on the baseline, the built-in font by its top-left. The trade-off to judge: taller/wider glyphs read better but fewer characters survive the 64px line-fit truncation in printJSon(). Each card also carries an A–Z / a–z showcase so every letter can be inspected on its own; those two rows are wrapped across as many 64px strips as they need rather than truncated, so no glyph is hidden. The swim/rest rows below them still truncate at 64px, because that is what the panel actually does. Entries badged custom are not Adafruit fonts: they are hand-drawn in viewer/gen-font-options.py and also emitted as fonts/Org3x5.h, fonts/Seg3x5.h, fonts/Seg5x7.h and fonts/Panel3x5.h for the firmware. Seg3x5/Seg5x7 are seven-segment (calculator-LED) designs. Panel3x5 is the hybrid of the other two: Seg3x5's digits with Org3x5's letters, punctuation and symbols. It is also emitted standalone as viewer/hub75-panel-font.js (window.PANEL_FONT) so a separate emulator page can load it with a plain <script src>.
