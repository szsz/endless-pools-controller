Small pixel fonts — number legibility on a 64×64 HUB75 panel

Every Adafruit_GFX font small enough for a 64px-tall list (glyph height ≤ 8px), plus the built-in 6×8 GLCD font as a baseline. Rendered with the same glyph decoder hub75.cpp uses — GFX fonts are placed on the baseline, the built-in font by its top-left. The trade-off to judge: taller/wider glyphs read better but fewer characters survive the 64px line-fit truncation in printJSon().
